Recover a failing data import

an inherited importer works in a simple demo but loses or duplicates records when a run is interrupted. Work through the failures, test your explanations, and leave a repair someone else can verify.

Time10-hour cap, including notes and a 45-minute live session.
PaymentPaid for time spent at your Hirexe hourly rate.
WindowAbout one week from receiving the materials.
SubmitPrivate ZIP/link with code and notes; no public solution PR.

The challenge

A local service imports pages of synthetic records. Repair it so temporary failures, repeated pages, and a stopped-and-restarted run do not silently lose or duplicate records. Failed runs must report their state rather than claim success or retry forever.

A quick fix is not the finish line. Verify it against every supplied failure scenario. Preserve the data rules and test expectations; do not disable the difficult cases to get a passing result.

What to do

  1. Diagnose. Reproduce a failure and test an explanation. After about two working hours, send a short checkpoint: run command, observed result, current hypothesis, and next experiment.
  2. Repair and challenge. Make a focused change, then run the remaining scenarios. At about five hours, send the current commit, evidence of progress, and what changed in your understanding. Submit early if finished.
  3. Close the loop. Add regression tests and prove the final records match the supplied expected results. Explain unresolved failures and your best next step. Reserve 45 minutes of the cap for the live session.

Hand back

Code, tests, and brief notes kept as you work: hypothesis → experiment → result → next decision. Include reproducible commands, actual results, time spent, and AI use. We value verified progress and a candid partial result over an unsupported claim of completion.

When a fix does not hold

Use the new evidence to choose your next test. Ask for clarification or a hint if needed; useful help-seeking is part of the work. AI is welcome, but verify its suggestions. There is no required number of failed attempts or reward for exceeding the cap.

Materials & boundaries

We will supply a runnable repo, local API simulator, failure scenarios, exact data/recovery rules, and a short reference guide. No live integrations or client data. Environment problems should be reported, not endured.

Live: investigate together

We will run one new combination of the documented failure conditions. Take time to inspect it, explain the evidence, and choose a next step. If your repair already handles it, show why. This adds no new business rule.

We are interested in how you investigate, adapt, communicate, and follow through, not just the final demo. Dates, tools, and access will be agreed before you begin.
